✿ Flaura: Flower ID

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Flaura identifies flowers on your device. Your photos are not uploaded to, stored by, or shared with us.

Information we handle

Flaura lets you take a photo with your camera or choose one from your photo library. The selected image is used only to run flower identification locally on your device.

How your photos are used

  • Photos are processed on-device by the app’s bundled machine-learning model.
  • Flaura does not transmit photos, identification results, or related metadata to a server.
  • Flaura does not create user accounts and does not collect names, email addresses, location data, contacts, or payment information.

Permissions

Camera permission is requested only when you choose to take a photo. Photo-library permission is requested only when you choose an existing photo. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Data sharing and retention

Because Flaura does not collect your personal data or photos, we do not sell, rent, share, or retain them. Your selected photo remains under your control on your device.

Children’s privacy

Flaura does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version will always be available at this page, with its update date shown above.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email listed on the Flaura app’s Google Play or Apple App Store listing.
